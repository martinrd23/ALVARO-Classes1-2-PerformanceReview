The strategic goal should therefore be to spend a few extra seconds verifying context when two answers look possible, rather than trying to become even faster.

Current advantage

You have a large time reserve in short grammar sets. You can later use that time for harder vocabulary items, reading passages, and final checking.

Current risk

Very fast answering can turn a structure you already know into a careless error. Your Week 1 feedback already suggested slowing down when two tenses appear plausible.

The short timed sets are less demanding than a full 50-question exam, so these pace figures should be treated as evidence of comfortable speed — not as a literal projection of full-exam completion time.
